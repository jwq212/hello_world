# InkCinema Visual Bible

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Stable

---

# Purpose

This document defines the universal visual language of InkCinema.

Every Agent must follow this specification.

The purpose of this document is visual consistency across every generated project.

Visual style is never determined by a single Agent.

It is determined by this Bible.

---

# Core Philosophy

Chinese aesthetics before visual spectacle.

Emotion before decoration.

Meaning before complexity.

Silence before noise.

Elegance before exaggeration.

Nature before artificiality.

---

# Artistic Direction

InkCinema follows the visual philosophy of

Traditional Chinese Landscape Painting

combined with

Modern Cinematic Documentary.

Target feeling

Elegant

Natural

Quiet

Timeless

Poetic

Immersive

Never flashy.

Never noisy.

Never overloaded.

---

# Historical Principle

Historical authenticity is mandatory.

Never mix dynasties.

Never introduce modern objects.

Never use architecture inconsistent with the selected historical period.

Unknown historical details must remain neutral instead of invented.

---

# Color Philosophy

Preferred colors

Ink Black

Jade Green

Bamboo Green

Rice White

Warm Earth

Soft Gold

Morning Blue

Moon Silver

Avoid

Neon

Pure RGB colors

Highly saturated gradients

Cyberpunk palettes

Excessive contrast

Every frame should appear naturally photographed.

---

# Light Philosophy

Light should describe time.

Light should describe emotion.

Light should never become the subject.

Morning

Soft warm light

Afternoon

Balanced natural sunlight

Sunset

Golden warm light

Night

Moonlight

Lantern

Fire

Fog should diffuse light.

Never create dramatic Hollywood lighting.

---

# Camera Philosophy

Camera should observe.

Camera should not perform.

Preferred

Slow Push

Slow Pull

Static

Gentle Pan

Gentle Tilt

Long Take

Natural handheld (minimal)

Avoid

Fast zoom

Action camera

Excessive drone

Shaky movement

Constant camera motion

The audience should forget the camera exists.

---

# Composition

Prefer

Negative Space

Foreground

Middle Ground

Background

Balanced framing

Natural symmetry

Visual breathing room

Avoid

Crowded composition

Extreme close-up without narrative purpose

Artificial framing

---

# Motion

Everything moves gently.

Wind

Slow

Trees

Soft

Clouds

Gradual

Water

Continuous

Grass

Subtle

Animals

Natural

Motion should never distract from emotion.

---

# Environment

Nature dominates.

Architecture integrates with nature.

Mountains

Mist

Water

Trees

Stone

Wood

Paper

Cloth

Natural materials first.

---

# Weather

Weather expresses emotion.

Sunny

Hope

Fog

Reflection

Rain

Loneliness

Snow

Silence

Wind

Freedom

Weather should support literature rather than replace it.

---

# Character Design

Characters should appear ordinary.

Beauty comes from authenticity.

Avoid

Fantasy armor

Game aesthetics

Anime exaggeration

Fashion photography

Historical clothing must remain believable.

---

# Emotion

Visual language should express

Calm

Reflection

Wonder

Longing

Hope

Humility

Respect

Never melodrama.

Never excessive facial expression.

---

# Rhythm

Every film should breathe.

Fast editing is discouraged.

Allow pauses.

Allow silence.

Allow stillness.

The audience needs time to observe.

---

# Shot Duration

Recommended

Wide Shot

6–12 seconds

Medium Shot

4–8 seconds

Close-up

3–6 seconds

Only shorten duration when narrative requires.

---

# Camera Height

Default

Human eye level.

Bird-eye view

Rare.

Ground-level

Only when storytelling benefits.

Camera height should feel natural.

---

# Lens Language

Preferred feeling

35mm

50mm

85mm

Avoid exaggerated ultra-wide distortion.

Perspective should remain realistic.

---

# Visual Continuity

Every project must maintain consistency for

Lighting

Weather

Architecture

Character appearance

Vegetation

Season

Time of day

Color palette

If inconsistency exists

Consistency wins.

---

# Forbidden Elements

Science fiction interfaces

Modern city skylines

Cars

Electric lights

Plastic

Glass skyscrapers

Modern logos

Modern typography

Modern roads

Digital devices

Unless the project explicitly requires them.

---

# Preferred References

Chinese Ink Painting

Song Dynasty Landscape Painting

Tang Dynasty Murals

Palace Museum references

Dunhuang Murals

BBC Documentary

CCTV Documentary

National Geographic Documentary

The visual language should feel documentary first and cinematic second.

---

# Final Principle

Every frame should be beautiful enough to become a standalone painting.

Every sequence should feel like reading a classical Chinese poem.

The audience should remember the emotion before remembering the technology.
